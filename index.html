How to get the best audio (read this once)

Browsers can't read audio straight out of a YouTube player, so Booth listens through your default microphone input. Two ways to feed it:

  • Easiest: play the video through your speakers and keep your mic nearby. Works, but quality depends on your room.
  • Cleanest: route system audio into the mic input with a virtual cable (VB-Cable on Windows, BlackHole on Mac, a loopback source on Linux) and set it as your default input. Use headphones so nothing echoes.
